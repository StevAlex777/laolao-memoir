# Surface: 姥姥的回忆录 app (index.html, all views)

Mode: Operate. Operator = the user's girlfriend on her Mac laptop beside 姥姥; Chrome, light room. Jobs: ask, record, fix transcript, capture spontaneous memories, end session (timeline + backup); timeline review; book generation/print; settings/backup. Must work without a Claude key (question bank) and with one (AI).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is a piece of 磁州窑 from 姥姥's own Handan: white slip ground, iron-black brushwork, ochre accent. Refuses the cream-paper + serif + terracotta memoir default and the grey-tool default.

OWN-WORLD: Slip white #f3f2ee ground; iron black-brown #2a1d16 for the rim header, ink and outlines (2px); ochre #9c5a24 for the active/primary; glaze grey #d9d3c7 for the era panel. Pill shapes and 2px iron outlines like painted bands; LXGW WenKai (霞鹜文楷) display — chosen over ZCOOL XiaoWei, which rendered 回 as tofu in the brand and cannot be trusted with AI-written questions; WenKai covers GB2312+ and keeps a brush-written character — PingFang UI, Noto Serif SC for her words. The printed book is her words only: era context stays on screen. Icons are authored SVG in one 2px stroke.

STORY: She sees one big question, presses one dark mic, watches 姥姥's words appear, saves, and the next question follows 姥姥's thread; anything 姥姥 suddenly remembers has its own button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Iron rim header (title left, tabs, backup status right) with an ochre underline band. Left work column: stage band as one outlined pill strip (done = iron fill, current = ochre); question at ~52px display; tool pills (读给姥姥听 / 换个问题 / 姥姥想起别的事); 120px iron mic ringed in ochre beside a white rounded transcript well; ochre primary save pill. Right 420px glaze panel 那一年 with slip cards outlined in iron.

FORM: chosen IMPECCABLE'S PICK (my #1 grounded candidate: 磁州窑 white-ground black-painted ware); seed:b264fd5407e45f92
